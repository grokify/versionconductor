## [v0.4.0] - 2026-08-02

### Highlights

- **Cedar-based policy engine**: dependency PR quarantine/approval decisions are now evaluated against Cedar policy bundles, with structured decision outcomes and human-readable comment formatting
- **GitHubCollector, GitHubMerger, GitHubReleaser, and the dependency graph Builder migrated off go-github onto `gogithub/clientv1.Client`**; `github.com/google/go-github` is now only a transitive dependency
- **GitHub App and `GITHUB_TOKEN` authentication** for CI usage, plus structured logging via `omniobserve`
